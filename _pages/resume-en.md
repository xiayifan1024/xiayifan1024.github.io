## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Electronic Information at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Miao Zhang. Previously, I worked as an engineer at the Key Laboratory of Intelligent Information Processing, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, focusing on visual recognition algorithms, model optimization, and deployment. I received my master's degree in Electronic Information from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, with research focused on digital signal processing and remote sensing image processing. I received my B.Eng. in Remote Sensing Science and Technology from China University of Mining and Technology, Beijing.

My current interests center on **spatial intelligence, inference acceleration, and edge intelligence**. In particular, I am exploring how to combine spatiotemporal changes in video with language semantics to retain key visual information under limited compute and memory budgets.

## Research Interests

- **Spatial Intelligence**: Spatial understanding of long-form and streaming video; integrating generation and understanding for spatial intelligence.
- **Inference Acceleration**: Model compression, token pruning, and KV cache compression.
- **Edge Intelligence**: Hardware-software co-design, service scheduling, and inference system architectures.
